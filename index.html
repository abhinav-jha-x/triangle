ABOUT TRIANGLE

More Than Education. A Foundation for Your Future.

TRIANGLE is a premier educational institute established in 2022 with a vision to transform the learning experience and help students achieve academic excellence.

Founded by three passionate educators — Shivam Mishra, Anup Kumar Thakur, and Madan Mohan — TRAINGLE brings together experience, knowledge, and a shared commitment to quality education.

Our institute provides comprehensive academic preparation for JEE, NEET, Board Examinations, and Foundation Courses. We believe education should go beyond textbooks and memorization: our teaching emphasizes conceptual clarity, analytical thinking, problem-solving, regular practice, and individual academic development.

Our goal is to create a supportive and motivating environment where students can discover their potential, build confidence, and work towards their dreams.

Our Vision

To become a trusted educational institution that inspires students to think independently and achieve academic excellence.

Our Mission

To provide quality education through expert teaching, conceptual learning, and structured academic preparation.

Our Philosophy

We believe that strong concepts, consistent practice, and proper mentorship are the foundations of success.

What is TRIANGLE?

TRIANGLE is an educational institute in Ranchi offering JEE, NEET, Board and Foundation preparation.

When was TRIANGLE established?

TRAINGLE was established in 2022.

Who are the founders and directors?

Shivam Mishra (Co-Founder & Director, Mathematics), Anup Kumar Thakur (Co-Founder & Director, Physics) and Madan Mohan (Co-Founder & Director, Chemistry).

Which courses does TRIANGLE offer?

JEE, NEET, Board Examination and Foundation courses.

Does TRIANGLEE offer JEE preparation?

Yes. The JEE program covers Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

Does TRIANGLE provide NEET preparation?

Yes. The NEET program covers Physics, Chemistry and Biology.

Are board examination courses available?

Yes, with concept-based teaching, subject-wise preparation, revision sessions and practice.

Mathematics, Science, Logical Reasoning and Conceptual Development.
